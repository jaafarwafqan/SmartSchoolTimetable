# ADR 0053: The owner's generation rules — daily limit levels, the teacher's day, balanced days, fair last periods

- Status: Accepted (owner decisions before the final tag, branch `work/phase-5`)
- Date: 2026-10-16

## Context
The owner decided seven generation rules before v1.0.0. All of them had to keep the performance gate and every quality gate. The model stays one weighted CP-SAT solve (ADR 0002, PHASE_4_MVP).

## Decisions
1. **Daily limit (H9) in three levels.** The subject's limit, then a grade's limit, then a section's limit, most specific first (`DailyCaps`, one class for the solver, the verifier and the validator). Each level is 1 to 6 lessons a day or «تلقائي». «تلقائي» means inherit; at the top it means automatic, max(2, ⌈weekly ÷ days⌉). The overrides are an owned table `SubjectCapOverrides (SubjectId, Scope, ScopeId, Cap)` with a unique key. A grade or section override of an archived grade or section is not sent to the solver. `SchedulingInput.CapOverrides` is hashed and left out of the JSON when empty, so the hash of a school without overrides keeps its shape. The readiness error `SUBJECT_DAYS_SHORT` already covers "limit × available days < weekly lessons" and now reads the resolved limit.
2. **«أقصى حصص متتالية للمعلم»: a strong priority (S9, «مهم جداً») by default, hard (H11) by choice; 2 or 3 only.** Owner's follow-up decision (2026-10-16): as a default hard rule it made every primary school with class teachers (24–29 lessons in their own section) impossible. The guide's fictional school timed out with no timetable, and the solver could not prove why. So it is now a priority: a window of k + 1 consecutive lesson numbers all taught by the teacher costs the weight. «إلزامي لكل المعلمين» (`SchedulingProfile.ConsecutiveHard`) or «إلزامي لهذا المعلم» (`Teacher.MaxConsecutiveHard`) makes it hard (H11) for everyone or for one teacher. The result notes list every teacher over the limit, with the day and the length of the run (`TimetableScore.ConsecutiveRuns`). When a run with some hard teacher **times out with no timetable**, the engine tries once more with the rule as a priority. If that finds a timetable, the owner is told «السبب على الأرجح …» (`CONSECUTIVE_LIKELY_CAUSE`, naming the hard teachers). Nothing is saved, because that timetable would break a rule the owner made hard. The readiness finding `TEACHER_CONSECUTIVE_SHORT` is an error only when the rule is hard for that teacher; as a priority it is a warning that says the limit will be exceeded. Migration `Phase5ConsecutivePriority` adds the two switches and the rule (on, 40).
   The original decision, kept for the hard mode: The school default (`SchedulingProfile.DefaultMaxConsecutive`, default 3) applies unless a teacher has his own (`Teacher.MaxConsecutive`). Lessons are consecutive when their lesson numbers follow each other in one shift on one day, even across a break row: the owner asked for free periods between lessons, and only this definition matches his formula. Model: in every window of k + 1 lesson numbers, at most k. Verifier code `TEACHER_CONSECUTIVE`. Diagnostics family `teacherConsecutive` (`CORE_TEACHER_CONSECUTIVE`).
   Readiness `TEACHER_CONSECUTIVE_SHORT` (an error, and a proof). In a run of m free lesson numbers a teacher can teach at most m − ⌊m ÷ (k + 1)⌋ lessons, which is the owner's ⌊(P + 1)·k ÷ (k + 1)⌋ for a full day (P = 6: 4 at k = 2, 5 at k = 3). The finding is raised only when this rule, and not the day or week maximum, is what makes the load impossible. It suggests k = 3 while k = 2.
   A hand-built input without a profile default has no H11, so earlier tests and witness-based property tests are unchanged.
3. **S6 «توزيع حصص المعلم بفواصل» replaces S2 as the default.** S2 («متتالية», `avoidTeacherGaps`) stays and is off by default. The two are mutually exclusive: the domain refuses both on, and the screen switches one off when the other is chosen. Penalty: per teacher, day and shift, each pair of lesson numbers n and n + 1 he teaches. A pair made of two double-lesson lessons does not count, so S6 never fights S5.
4. **S7 «توزيع أيام المعلم بالتساوي», weight 40 («مهم جداً»), with an option to make it hard (H12, `SchedulingProfile.BalanceHard`).** `TeacherDays` gives each teacher's usable slots per day. The days with the most usable slots are his full days, and they must stay within one lesson of each other: penalty max − min − 1, not below 0. A partly available day may take at most its proportional share, ⌈load × usable ÷ total usable⌉. Hard mode enforces both. Readiness `TEACHER_BALANCE_HARD_SHORT` is an error only when the hard rule makes the load impossible.
5. **S8 «عدالة الحصص الأخيرة», weight 20 («مهم»).** The last period of a shift on a day is the highest lesson number any section with a curriculum has there. A teacher's share of the weighted last periods is the school's total shared out by weekly load, rounded up. The penalty is what he holds above it. A day that is an evening session in some semester (R3 mapping) weighs 2, a morning day 1. The evening days are **not hashed** (`NotHashed`): a soft preference never makes a saved timetable out of date, which keeps DECISIONS_PENDING #81. First periods and the second-to-last period are not weighed (the owner did not need them). The result note names the teacher with the most and the one with the fewest, among teachers with at least half the average load (`TimetableScore.LastPeriods`).
6. **Corrective migration `Phase5GenerationRules`.** It adds the new columns, the override table and the check constraints (`DailyCap` 1–6, `MaxConsecutive` 2 or 3, `DefaultMaxConsecutive` 2 or 3, `Cap` 1–6). It also fixes an existing school's priorities:
   - It adds the three new rules at their defaults.
   - It switches «متتالية» off when it still has the old default (on, 30); otherwise the school's own choice is kept and the new spread rule is added off.
   - It raises the profile version, so a saved timetable shows «تغيّرت أولويات الجدول».

## Consequences
- The objective is larger: more terms. On the gate schools the first timetable came in 2.0–2.3 s (24/40, 2 workers), and in under 1 s on the owner-shaped school (docs/PERFORMANCE.md). No run proved optimality in 60 s, as before.
- The scorer and the solver define every rule identically; for a proven-optimal timetable the scorer's total equals the objective (tested per rule).
- Evening weighting depends on the R3 mapping, which is not hashed, so two runs with the same hash can weigh evening days differently if the mapping changed in between. This is accepted: the mapping changes no hard rule.
