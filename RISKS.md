# Risks

Likelihood and impact are Low / Medium / High. Status: **Open** (not mitigated), **Mitigated** (reduced, residual remains), **Accepted** (owner or documented decision), **Closed**. Seeded from the phase reports; update it at the end of every milestone.

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **H3 solvability:** the rule "lessons start at period 1 with no gaps" makes some small or unusual curricula infeasible | Medium | High | Diagnostics name the conflicting teachers, section, resource and rule. M3 measures realistic cases and, if H3 blocks them, proposes an option (not implemented without approval) | Open | `docs/PHASE4_REPORT.md` §6, `docs/PERFORMANCE.md` |
| R2 | **Large schools untested:** more than 24 real sections, or tight teacher availability, are unmeasured. Synthetic 40/54 found a first timetable in about 3.4 s, one seed | Medium | High | M3 performance gate (40 sections / ~54 teachers, tighter availability, double-shift) reported honestly; two-stage solve and decomposition stay as documented proposals M3 (2026-10-11) measured the published program with a 512 MB heap and 2 logical processors: 24 sections first timetable 7.7 s, 40 sections 11.2 s (`docs/M3_RELEASE_VALIDATION_AR.md`). Real data with tight availability is still unmeasured. | Mitigated (measured, residual remains) | `docs/PERFORMANCE.md` |
| R3 | **Paper printing not verified:** print output was checked through print-media emulation, not on paper | Medium | Medium | M2 verifies A4 landscape with Playwright `page.pdf` on the print route and views it; owner prints one page on the school printer during acceptance Print checked as rendered PDF pages in `m2-printing.spec.ts` and the guide; the paper test is in `docs/OWNER_TEST_SCRIPT_V1_AR.md`. | Open (owner prints on paper) | `docs/PHASE4_REPORT.md` §6 |
| R4 | **Upgrade path:** a school database from an older schema must migrate without data loss | Medium | High | Corrective migrations only; backup restored from the previous schema is tested; M2 adds an automatic backup before migrating, automatic rollback on failure and `docs/UPGRADE_AR.md` Done in M2: pre-upgrade copy, rollback, Arabic startup message; the installer upgrade is tested (`m5-installer.spec.ts`). | Mitigated | `docs/PHASE4G_REPORT.md` |
| R5 | **Literary curriculum totals** from the owner's source do not add up for three stages; some Iraqi curriculum numbers and break lengths are suggestions, not official values | High | Medium | Rows kept as supplied, totals computed, stages flagged, readiness reports the mismatch; owner reviews | Open | `docs/DECISIONS_PENDING.md` #40, #41 |
| R6 | **Pre-solve validator is deliberately incomplete:** conflicts needing combined reasoning are found only by the solver | Medium | Low | The solver's infeasibility diagnostics; the validator never claims readiness it cannot prove | Accepted | ADR 0035 |
| R7 | **Greedy assignment suggester** can leave lines unassigned when a different global assignment would fit | Medium | Low | Each unassigned line shows a reason; the owner assigns by hand | Accepted | `docs/PHASE3_REPORT.md` |
| R8 | **Data loss from a user mistake:** wrong restore, deleted backup, restore over newer data | Medium | High | Two confirmations, automatic pre-restore copy, no backup is ever deleted by the app; M2 adds integrity check and a restore confirmation that states what is replaced | Mitigated | ADR 0042 |
| R9 | **Database not encrypted:** a person with access to the computer can read the SQLite file | Medium | Medium | Single local owner, loopback only, no encryption claim made; SQLCipher GO/NO-GO decided in M3 | Accepted (SQLCipher NO-GO, ADR 0010) | ADR 0010 |
| R10 | **Localhost attacks:** a malicious web page calling the local API (cross-origin, DNS rebinding) | Low | High | Strict Host/Origin validation, SameSite=Strict cookie, per-launch token, no wildcard CORS; automated tests; M3 repeats them against the published folder `LocalAttackSuiteTests` covers every state-changing route. | Mitigated | `SECURITY.md`, `LocalApiTests` |
| R11 | **Dependency licences and vulnerabilities:** `npm audit` reports six high findings in the dev-only `stylelint` chain; QuestPDF licence was never reviewed | Low | Medium | Production audit is clean; CI audits per policy; no server PDF (QuestPDF not used, M2 ADR) | Accepted | `docs/DECISIONS_PENDING.md` #44 |
| R12 | **Single point of failure:** one owner account; a lost password and recovery code cannot be recovered | Low | High | Recovery code shown once with print guidance; regeneration with the current password; no alternate recovery by design | Accepted | ADR 0009 |
| R13 | **Release folder runs only on Windows x64** and was observed on one machine; the launcher's browser step was not seen on screen | Medium | Medium | M3 runs the published folder on a temporary database; the final guide pipeline runs it end to end The installer, launcher, WebView2 and browser fall-back are tested here (`m5-installer.spec.ts`); not tested on a clean VM, Windows 10 1809 or a 4 GB PC. | Mitigated (not seen on a clean machine) | `docs/PHASE4_REPORT.md` §6 |
| R14 | **Screenshot baselines are machine-specific** (Windows/Chromium); CI fonts or rendering may differ | Medium | Low | CI runs on `windows-latest` and uploads diffs on failure; baselines are re-generated only after viewing them | Open | M0 CI |
| R15 | **Unsigned installer:** Windows SmartScreen warns on a program without a paid code-signing certificate | High | Low | The guide (chapter 1) shows «More info» → «Run anyway»; the SHA-256 file lets the owner check the file; a certificate is a possible later purchase | Accepted | ADR 0052 |
| R16 | **32-bit Windows and Windows 10 older than 1809 are refused; machines with less than 4 GB are slow** | Low | Medium | The installer refuses with an Arabic message and warns below about 3.4 GB; the guide states the requirements | Accepted | ADR 0052 |
| R17 | **Teacher mobile app (Phase 9) is out of scope for 1.0.0**: no phone access by design (loopback only) | Low | Low | Recorded as a future idea only; nothing in 1.0.0 depends on it | Accepted | `DELIVERY_PLAN.md` |
